# GS AUTOBILAN — Staff quick guide (V1)

**For:** Agency Admin desk staff · **Admin:** https://gsautobilan.com/admin

---

## What the website does (and does not do)

| Does | Does not |
|------|----------|
| Takes **booking requests** online | Auto-confirm appointments |
| Gives customers a **reference number** | Send SMS/WhatsApp automatically |
| Lets customers **track** appointment + documents | Show live inspection / lane status |
| Stores **contact messages** for follow-up | Replace phone/WhatsApp confirmation |

**Rule:** Online booking = **request received**. You **confirm by phone or WhatsApp**.

---

## When a customer books online

1. Customer submits form on **Rendez-vous** page.
2. They receive a reference like **`GS-2026-000123`**.
3. Booking appears in admin as **New Request**.
4. **You call or WhatsApp** the customer to confirm date/time.
5. In admin, update:
   - **Status** → Confirmed (or Rescheduled / Cancelled)
   - **Confirmed date & time**
   - **Public message** (visible on tracking page)
6. Update **Document readiness** when papers are checked:
   - Not reviewed → Documents complete → Missing info → Ready for visit

---

## How customers track their request

Page: **Suivi rendez-vous** (`/fr/suivi-rendez-vous`)

They need **all three**:

1. Reference number (`GS-2026-…`)
2. Phone number (same as booking)
3. Vehicle registration (plate)

If one is wrong, the system shows a generic “not found” message — do not reveal whether a reference exists.

---

## Contact messages

1. Messages arrive in **Contact messages** in admin.
2. Read, respond by phone/email/WhatsApp offline.
3. Mark status: New → In review → Responded → Closed.

Agency Admins see messages for **their agency** only.
